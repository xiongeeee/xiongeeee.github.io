I'm a second-year EngD student at Hefei University of Technology. My research interests include machine learning, probabilistic methods, uncertainty quantification, and AI for Science, with a particular focus on weather forecasting.

I received my bachelor's degree from the School of Mathematics and Statistics at Anhui Normal University.

During my undergraduate studies, I received the following national awards:

- Second Prize, Undergraduate Group, Contemporary Undergraduate Mathematical Contest in Modeling, sponsored by Higher Education Press (高教社杯全国大学生数学建模大赛本科生组国家二等奖)
- Second Prize, Undergraduate Group, National Market Survey and Analysis Competition (全国市场调查与分析大赛本科生组国家二等奖)
- Third Prize, Undergraduate Group, National English Competition for College Students (全国大学生英语竞赛本科生组国家三等奖)
